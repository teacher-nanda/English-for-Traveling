Travel tips

Your first minutes in the U.S.: what to expect

A simple guide to passport control for visitors

When you land in the U.S., you first go to passport control. A border officer checks your passport and your visa or travel authorization. You may also give your fingerprints and have your photo taken.

The officer usually asks a few simple questions. Why are you visiting? Where will you stay? How long will you stay? Are you traveling alone? The questions are short. Your answers can be short, too.

Stay calm and polite, even if the officer is serious. Have your documents ready: your passport, your hotel booking, and your return ticket. It is a good idea to have them on your phone and on paper.

Always tell the truth. Your answers should match your documents. For example, if you say you are here on vacation, do not carry papers for a job interview.

If you do not understand a question, ask the officer to repeat it. That is normal. After passport control, you pick up your bags and go through customs. Do not bring forbidden items, and tell the officer about food or a lot of cash.
