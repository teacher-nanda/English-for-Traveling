TravelerHi. We'd like two tickets for today, please. Is the line always this long?
AgentIn summer it's busier than in winter. Timed-entry tickets are faster, but the next ones are for noon.
TravelerNoon is fine. Is it worth getting the audio guide?
AgentDefinitely. It's the best way to hear the stories, and it costs only five euros.
TravelerGreat, we'll take two. Is there a guided tour in English?
AgentYes, at one o'clock. It's longer than the audio guide, but the guides are very good.
TravelerPerfect. And is the castle as crowded in the afternoon?
AgentNo, it's usually quieter after three. The tower has the most beautiful view, so go up before it closes.
TravelerThanks. Is there a sightseeing pass for the other museums?
AgentThere is. It's cheaper than buying separate tickets if you visit three or more.
TravelerThen we'll think about it. How much is everything?
AgentThat's forty-two euros for the two tickets and the audio guides.

📐 Grammar

How we build them

Short adjectives (one syllable) take -er / the -est.
Longer adjectives take more / the most.
Remember the irregular ones: good → better → the best; bad → worse → the worst.

Thing 1The tower
Comparativeis taller than
Thing 2the castle.

Comparative and superlative

Shorttall → taller than → the tallest
Longcrowded → more crowded than → the most crowded
Irregulargood → better → the best

as… as

EqualThe park is as big as a town.
Not equalIt's not as crowded as the castle.

Travel advice

How to beat the crowds

Smart ways to see famous sights without the long lines

Few things ruin a trip like waiting two hours to see something for ten minutes. The good news is that a little planning can save you a lot of time.

First, check each sight's rules before you go. Many popular attractions now use timed-entry tickets, and it is smart to book as soon as your dates are firm. Some places sell out weeks in advance.

Second, choose your day and time carefully. Free-entry days are usually the most crowded, and many museums close on certain days, which pushes everyone into the places that are open. Go early in the morning or late in the afternoon, when tour groups and cruise passengers are not there.

Third, look for less busy ways in. Some attractions have a second entrance, and a guided tour often lets you skip part of the line. Sightseeing passes and combination tickets can also save time and money.

Finally, remember that the best moments are often at less famous places. Ask locals about alternatives, and visit sights that few tourists know about.

Sources: Rick Steves
