💭 Check your understanding

  • “This jacket is too small.” Can you wear it? No. It is smaller than you need.
  • “This jacket isn't big enough.” Is the meaning the same as “too small”? Yes. Both mean the jacket is not the right size.

📐 Grammar

How we build them

too comes before the adjective. enough comes after the adjective, but before a noun.
In an if sentence, use the present simple after if, never will.

IfIf I buy two,
Resultwould you give me
Nouna discount?

too and enough

too + adjectiveIt's too expensive.
adjective + enoughIt isn't big enough.
enough + nounDo you have enough change?

If sentences

If + present, willIf you lower the price, I'll take it.
If + present, would you…?If I buy two, would you give me a discount?

Money tips

How to avoid losing money on your trip

Smart ways to pay and get cash when you travel abroad

Many travelers lose money without noticing it. Fees and bad exchange rates can quietly add up to a lot over a long trip.

First, look at your card. Some cards charge a foreign transaction fee on every purchase, often around three percent. Cards with no such fee are a much better choice abroad.

Second, when a shop or ATM asks whether you want to pay in your own currency or the local one, choose the local currency. The other option, called dynamic currency conversion, usually gives you a worse rate.

For cash, an ATM in a bank is generally better than a currency booth. Airport exchange booths are convenient but often add five to ten percent or more to the real rate. Some banks even repay ATM fees, so ask before you go.

Finally, always carry a backup card, in case one is lost or blocked. And before leaving, think about what to do with your leftover currency.

Sources: One Mile at a Time
